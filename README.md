🏥 Al Anwar Medical Equipment
Engineered for Recovery. Designed for Life.
https://mansiwadile11.github.io/Al-anwar-medical/#

A modern, responsive healthcare e-commerce website concept for Al Anwar Medical Equipment Trading Co. L.L.C., focused on orthopedic, rehabilitation, mobility, and medical support products across the UAE.

The website combines a premium visual design with interactive shopping features, product discovery, responsive navigation, and specialist assistance through WhatsApp.

🎯 Main Sections
Hero

The website provides a visual way for users to find products based on their condition or area of discomfort.

Knee & Joint

♿ Accessibility

Accessibility considerations included in the project:

Semantic navigation
ARIA labels
ARIA-expanded states
Keyboard-friendly menus
Focus-visible outlines
Skip-to-content link

Escape-key cart closing
